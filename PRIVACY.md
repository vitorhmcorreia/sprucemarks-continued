# Privacy Policy: Sprucemarks Continued

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Sprucemarks Continued sorts your bookmarks (favorites in Microsoft Edge) inside your browser. It does not collect,
store, transmit, share, or sell any personal or usage data.

- **Bookmarks permission:** used only to read and reorder your bookmarks, according to the options you choose.
  Bookmark data never leaves your browser.
- **Storage permission:** used only to save your options and the extension's own state in the browser's local
  extension storage on this device. Nothing is synced or sent anywhere.
- **Dead Links (only when you use it):** when you click Check Links, and only after you grant website access, your
  browser contacts each bookmarked website directly to see whether the page still exists. No cookies are sent with
  these checks, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else. You can revoke the access at any time on that page.
- **No other network requests:** apart from the checks above, the extension does not contact any server, has no
  analytics, ads, or tracking, and contains no remote code.

Questions or concerns: open an issue at https://github.com/vitorhmcorreia/sprucemarks-continued/issues.
